   - work at JEEViKA, the Bihar Rural Livelihoods Promotion Society;

   All of this needs her confirmation.

6. **Name collisions.**
   - "Culture Shock" is a crowded name: a UK drum & bass DJ, a UK ska-punk band, podcasts, and US campus culture shows.
   - "Cultural Shock" is also an apparel brand.
   - In India the acronym **CSP** already means the historic **Congress Socialist Party** (1934). That is a problem given the Congress claim above.
   - CSP will be read alongside the **Cockroach Janta Party (CJP)**, India's 2026 Gen Z "satire party" movement, whose protests the founder has posted about.
