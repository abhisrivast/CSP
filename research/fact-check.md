# Fact-check report

Checked 24 October 2026 by an adversarial fact-check pass over the claims gathered in `research/`. Each claim was checked against its cited source, re-opened today wherever possible. A second independent source was used where one could be reached.

**Totals:** 234 claims checked. 15 corrected, 0 false, 11 unverifiable, 208 confirmed.

**Limits of this pass.** The web-search budget for this run was used up before checking began, so no new searches were possible. Everything below comes from fetching the cited URLs, plus a few known primary pages (Wikipedia, the Commons API, Wikidata, RDAP and official docs). Some sites blocked the fetcher: Amazon.in (503), weforum.org, feministerna.se, thefunambulist.net, merriam-webster.com and electoralcommission.org.uk (403). web.archive.org could not be reached at all. Claims that depend only on those sites are marked unverifiable. "Confirmed" means the cited source says it. It does not mean the claim is fit to print: see the notes.

This is research, not legal advice.
